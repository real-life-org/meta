# Coordination

The purpose from the developer-experience circle of 2026-09-08: one place where it is visible who works on what, and a log of what got done, so nobody has to remember to tell everyone.

**Everyone writes in their own language here.** This directory is the exception to the repository's English rule.

## Files

- `current-work.md`: one line per person, what they are on right now, updated by the person or by a process on their behalf.
- `log/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`: one file per day and topic. Written by a person after a call, or by a process from a transcript.

## Log entry format

Fixed blocks, so a process can write them and a person can skim them:

```
# YYYY-MM-DD · <topic>
Present: …

## Who works on what
## Direction set
## Onboarding questions        (only when a newcomer asked something)
## Open
## Contacts and opportunities   (only what may be public)
## What touches the roadmaps
```

Uncertain speaker attribution is marked with (?). Nothing personal, nothing about third parties beyond what they would agree to read here: this repository is public.
