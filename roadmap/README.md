## Rules

- A roadmap lists **what** and **why**, in the order it is meant to happen. Not how, not by whom, not by when unless a date is real.
- Each entry names the cell of the layer picture it belongs to, or the seam. If it fits neither, it belongs in another roadmap or is not a roadmap item.
- Progress is public; details are not. An entry may say "in progress" or a rough share; it never links internal notes.
- Open questions that block an entry are listed under it, as questions.
- Decisions taken are moved to a short "decided" section at the end with the date, never deleted.

The roadmaps start empty. They are filled from the open questions of the three worlds, in rounds, with the people who own them.
